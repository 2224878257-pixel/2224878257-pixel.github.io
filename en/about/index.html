How I approach acquisition

Platforms and tactics change. Sound business judgment does not. My work follows three principles.

01

Business before platform

Understand the market, customer, product and sales process before choosing media, content or operational fixes.

02

Bottleneck before budget

High costs are not always a traffic problem. Creative, targeting, pages, follow-up and closing can all be constraints.

03

Evidence before claims

No invented clients or unauthorized results—only explainable and verifiable evidence supports a conclusion.
